Project Engineering Notes Reference (From Your Hand-Written Documentation)

☀️ 1. Solar Panel & Photocurrent Specs

• Baseline STC: 1000 W/m2, 25°C → 50W (Vmpp = 18.0V, Impp = 2.78A, Rmpp = 6.47 Ω).
• Short Circuit: Isc = 2.92A • Open Circuit: Voc = 21.6V.
• Photocurrent: Iph = [Iph,ref + Ki(T − Tref)] × (G / Gref).
• Ipv is directly proportional to Irradiance G (1000 W/m2 → 2.78A, 500 W/m2 → 1.39A).

🔧 2. Boost Hardware Components (From Notes)

• 1000 μF Input Cap: In parallel with panel to stabilize DC rail and filter 10 kHz switching noise.
• 678 μH Inductor: Primary magnetic energy storage element (EL = ½LI2).
• Power MOSFET: Gate (g) receives 10 kHz PWM signal from MPPT algorithm.
• Schottky Diode: One-way valve with low forward voltage drop; prevents reverse flow.
• 470 μF Output Cap & 15 Ω Load: Smooths pulsating boosted voltage into steady DC supply.

⚡ 3. The 2-Phase Switching Cycle (How it Boosts)

• Phase 1: MOSFET is ON (Energy Storage):
PWM gate pulse = HIGH → MOSFET closes to GND. Current path: Panel (18V) → Inductor (678 μH) → MOSFET → GND. Inductor stores energy in magnetic field. Diode is reverse-biased (blocked); 470 μF capacitor discharges its own energy to supply 15 Ω load.
• Phase 2: MOSFET is OFF (Energy Transfer):
PWM gate pulse = LOW → MOSFET opens (GND disconnected). Inductor resists sudden current drop (current drop ko resist karta hai) → induced back-EMF adds on top of 18V (Voltage ↑↑↑ adds on to 18V ⇒ V > 18V). Diode turns ON (forward-biased) → current flows to output, recharging 470 μF cap & supplying 15 Ω load simultaneously.

🔋 4. Dual Load Stages & Impedance Matching

• Formula: Rin = Rload × (1 − D)2.
• Phase 1 (15 Ω Resistor): Setting D = 0.343 locks Rin = 6.47 Ω.
• Step Test (15 Ω → 30 Ω): Rin spikes to 12.95 Ω, voltage shoots to 21.6V, current chokes to 1.67A. MPPT increases D → 0.536 to re-match 6.47 Ω.
• Phase 2 (6S Battery): 19.5V–25.2V. Dynamic Reff = Vbat / Ibat (10 Ω depleted → 50.4 Ω full). D = 1 − 18 / Vbat adjusts continuously.
